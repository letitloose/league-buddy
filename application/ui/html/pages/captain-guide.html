New Captain's Guide

Everything below shows up right on your team's page and on each match's page — there's no separate admin area to go find. If you don't see a button described here, either the game hasn't been recorded as one of your team's yet, or a league admin controls that one instead.

Team Admin

On your team's page.

Invite Players — send an email invite to join your roster, or add someone already registered on another team via the roster picker.

Join Requests — approve or deny players who've asked to join your team on their own.

Import Roster — upload a CSV to add your whole roster at once instead of one player at a time. A sample file is linked right on that page.

Export Roster — download your current roster as a PDF.

Remove from Team — take a player off your roster.

Make/Remove Scorekeeper — hand match-day score entry to a trusted player, so someone can log goals and cards from the sideline even if you're playing yourself.

Edit Team — update your team's motto, home field, or established date.

Match Admin

On any match involving your team.

Edit Match — set or correct the date, time, location, and final score.

Goals & Cards — record who scored (and who assisted), own goals, and yellow/red cards.

Player of the Match — call out your standout performer.

Captain's Notes — a short note about the match, visible to anyone viewing it (short-handed, a rough pitch, whatever's worth recording).

Message for RSVP Reminders — a note that goes out with the automatic reminder your teammates get before the match (carpool plans, uniform color, anything time-sensitive). Use Send Test Reminder to preview it before it goes out for real.

RSVPs — see who's in and who's out for the match at a glance, right next to your team's name.

Questions about something not covered here? Reach out and we'll help you sort it out.
